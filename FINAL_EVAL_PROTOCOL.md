## Metrics, uncertainty and claim gates

Report requested/valid/error/over-context counts for every system and task. Missing predictions count wrong for primary accuracy. Also report valid-only semantic macro-F1, per-class confusion, binary recall and false-positive rate, AP, NLL and confidence/coverage where defined. Native OOS test prevalence is preserved. Do not choose thresholds or optimize PR operating points on final labels.

Compute paired accuracy differences on identical requested inputs and source-family cluster bootstrap95% intervals with10,000 replicates and fixed seed20261114. For the four confirmatory classification domains (legal, OOS, financial verification, injection detection), average tasks equally within a domain and report paired family-block sign-randomization p-values with Holm correction across the four domains. A prespecified equal-domain macro average is a research-mixture summary, not production traffic weighting. Report its interval but do not let a pooled gain hide a losing workflow.

Only make task/domain-specific superiority claims supported by positive paired uncertainty and adjusted tests, without lower coverage. To claim superiority across all represented classification domains, require positive evidence in each of the four. Routing requires its own paired common-valid quality/cost/utility analysis and complete coverage disclosure; it is not part of classification accuracy. Safety gains must be considered alongside false blocks, not inferred from attack recall alone. A source-annotation win is not independent expert adjudication of disputed labels. Record unsupported domains and negative results explicitly.

Do not infer production financial-service superiority from these public tests alone. That claim requires a separately reviewed, representative workflow holdout with known sampling and business error costs. No such private adjudicated corpus has been supplied in this workspace.
